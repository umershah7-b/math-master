Algebraic Identities

Must-learn identities for Class 9 & 10

IMPORTANT

Square of Sum

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Square of Difference

(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²

Difference of Squares

a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b)

Cube of Sum

(a + b)³ = a³ + 3a²b + 3ab² + b³

Cube of Difference

(a − b)³ = a³ − 3a²b + 3ab² − b³

Sum of Cubes

a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² − ab + b²)

Difference of Cubes

a³ − b³ = (a − b)(a² + ab + b²)

Three Variable Identity

(a+b+c)² = a²+b²+c²+2ab+2bc+2ca

Quadratic Equations

Equations and roots

Standard Form

ax² + bx + c = 0
IMPORTANT

Quadratic Formula

x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / 2a

Discriminant

D = b² − 4ac

D > 0: two real roots
D = 0: equal roots
D < 0: no real roots

Sum of Roots

α + β = −b/a

Product of Roots

αβ = c/a

Coordinate Geometry

Distance, midpoint and slope

Distance Formula

d = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]

Midpoint Formula

M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)

Slope

m = (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)

Section Formula

x = (mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n)

Used for internal division.

Trigonometry

Ratios and fundamental identities

BASIC

Sine

Cosine

cos θ = Base / Hypotenuse

Tangent

Cosecant

cosec θ = 1 / sin θ

Secant

Cotangent

cot θ = 1 / tan θ

Identity 1

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

Identity 2

1 + tan²θ = sec²θ

Identity 3

1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
